# Thesis Statement: 
This project takes a look into the air quality of Dallas, 
Texas more specifically the PM2.5 levels over time, highlighting the connection 
between the air quality and the health effects. By visualizing the changes of PM2.5 levels over the years, I hope to tell the story of how air quality can effect health. 

# Context for my Data Visualization: 
The focus of my project, PM2.5, or fine particulate matter, refers to particles smaller than 2.5 micrometers in diameter. The particles are a very significant air pollutant, impacting alot of Dallas' residents' health. In this project, I aim to visualize the historical data of PM2.5 levels over time and explore how the air quality has changed over time and how these shifts could be contributing to health problems. This project is especially important to me, as I've experienced the effects of asthma, and I hope to bring attention to the role the environment plays in health. 

# Data to be Visualized: 
The data used in my project comes from the Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) dataset containing records of PM2.5 levels over the years. There are several columns used to track the levels and understand the air quality, including: 

- Date: specific date that the PM2.5 levels were taken 
- Source: method of data collection 
- Daily Mean PM2.5 Concentration: The avg concentration of PM2.5 for each day 
- Units: units measured µg/m³
- Daily AQI Value: The AQI of the day
- Local Site Name: name of the local monitoring site where the data was collected

& more!
